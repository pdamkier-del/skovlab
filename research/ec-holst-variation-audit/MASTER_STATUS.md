# Holst variation audit — verified status for issue #6

**No physically controlled torsion-caused Level B bounce established.** The quadratic modified-Holst action does yield a healthy propagating pseudoscalaron. The plain linear term does not create that mode, although it changes the minimally coupled fermion contact.

## Established results

- Independent first-order tetrad/connection equations, all 24 connection components, direct affine/gamma tests and scalar reduction verified.
- Minimal linear-Holst contact: −3γ² A²/[16M²(1+γ²)]. Vacuum linear Holst reduces to GR; no new propagating scalar.
- For M²>0,c>0 in M²R̃/2+βH+cH², exact canonical scalar kinetic/potential and positive vacuum mass reproduced. Source equation/notation differences are documented rather than assumed equivalent.
- Healthy scalar plus complete NEC matter cannot bounce in flat/open FLRW. This is a conditional theorem, not a quantum-matter or all-EC no-go.
- Three closed k=+1 potential-dominated turns have finite metric and full torsionful curvature, regular two-sided proper-time evolution, independently checked original coframe equations and temporal convergence.
- A nonlinear inhomogeneous constrained S³ perturbation has independent constraint refinement and a positive local normal-congruence expansion derivative. Raw data are retained; no full nonlinear time evolution or global completeness is claimed.

The last two items are curvature-assisted scalar turns. The scalar is torsion-origin, but their metric mechanism is also present in torsion-free canonical-scalar GR, and a separately constrained positive-Λ closed control turns. That is not a demonstrated new NEC-evading torsion repulsion. Fermion quantum-state control, omitted operators, noise and the published intermediate trace-label dictionary remain unresolved.
