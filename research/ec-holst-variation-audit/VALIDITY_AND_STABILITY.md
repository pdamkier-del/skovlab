# Validity, stability and Level B assessment

For M²>0, real β and c>0, there is one canonical pseudoscalar in addition to the two graviton polarizations; no propagating trace/axial vector survives independent auxiliary connection elimination. The vacuum kinetic sign and m_φ² are positive. The canonical scalar principal sound speed is one around the bosonic background. A local negative V″ away from the vacuum would be a potential-growth issue, not a kinetic ghost. No full finite-density fermion stability analysis is supplied.

The illustrative c=10⁸ closed example has a light scalar and metric/full-connection curvature far below M² (multiply recorded dimensionless curvatures by m_φ², and curvature squares by m_φ⁴). Its torsion and reconstructed connection are finite through the checked interval. Low curvature is a necessary useful check, not proof of UV completeness or control of every omitted operator.

Keeping cH² nonperturbatively introduces a light mode explicitly. Additional aR̃² operators require |aR̃|/M²≪1 in the claimed regime; higher d_n H^n require |d_n H^(n−2)|≪c with appropriate dimensions. Neither a UV matching calculation nor uniform bounds on all coefficients were supplied by this audit. Large c alone is not a demonstrated pathology, but it does not fix the remaining EFT coefficients. Canonical field values also require their interactions/matching to remain under control; small Hubble rate alone is insufficient.

Minimal fermion derivative and contact coefficients obey |h|≤√(3/8)/M and 0≤g≤3/(16M²). That limits a proposed parametric enhancement; it does not establish a physical quantum state, small stress noise, anomaly matching or Pauli-compatible dense-matter bounce. Nonminimal sources change both interactions and their cutoff and need independent matching. The prior programme's quantum-source limitations remain valid in their stated scopes, not a universal bound on this entire theory.
