# Torsion elimination, linear Holst and Nieh–Yan

Direct affine contractions give, in the trace/axial sector,

R̃=R+2∇_μt^μ−(2/3)t²+q²/24,
H=∇_μq^μ−(2/3)t_μq^μ,
L_D=L_D°−q·A/8.

Tensor-torsion quadratic pieces must be included in the initial variation; the full 24-component system shows their source-free solution is zero for real parameters. The EH divergence has constant coefficient; the Holst divergence has coefficient f(z), so its bulk contribution is **−∂f·q**.

With X_μ=∂_μf+A_μ/8, the complete auxiliary bulk Lagrangian is

L_C=−M²t²/3+M²q²/48−(2f/3)t·q−X·q.

Its independent variations give

t_μ=−(f/M²)q_μ,
q^μ=24[∂^μf+A^μ/8]/[M²(1+16f²/M⁴)].

Substitution into the *whole* action yields

**L_C,on=−12(∂f+A/8)²/[M²(1+16f²/M⁴)].**

Dropping the Dirac connection term before substitution gives a wrong sign/coefficient. Adding another minimal-EC contact after this elimination double-counts it.

## Linear f=β

There is no scalar kinetic source and no extra bosonic propagating mode. With no fermions, torsion vanishes and the metric equations reduce to GR. With the declared minimally coupled Dirac action, however,

L_4ψ=−[3/(16M²)] [γ²/(1+γ²)] A²,  γ=M²/(4β).

Thus plain linear Holst **can change fermionic contact/bounce parameters**; it does not create a new scalar bounce mechanism. For real γ its magnitude never exceeds the minimal EC coefficient; γ→∞ recovers EC, and γ→0 suppresses this contact. A change of epsilon/chirality convention changes odd-source signs, not this even suppression factor. Complex γ=±i is not a physical parameter rescue in the chosen theory.

## Boundary distinction

The exact form identity is

d(e_a∧Tᵃ)=T_a∧Tᵃ−e_a∧e_b∧Fᵃᵇ
=T_a∧Tᵃ+(H/2)vol.

Consequently H vol=2[d(e_a∧Tᵃ)−T_a∧Tᵃ]. A linear Holst density is not merely a boundary term when torsion is present. A Nieh–Yan-completed action is a different specification from Holst alone. Likewise a flat-space total divergence, when covariantized with torsion, can change a trace-current coupling; its consequences are not removed by calling it a convention.

For an explicitly declared nonminimal source +t·J, elimination instead gives

L_C,on=3J²/(4M²)−12(∂f+A/8+(f/M²)J)²/(M²D).

At constant f, choosing J=2B A cancels the γ dependence of the AA coefficient exactly, giving −3A²/(16M²). This is an algebraically verified *changed matter coupling*, not γ-independence of the original minimal Dirac model.
