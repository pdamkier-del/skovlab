## Convention safeguards

- Signature \((-+++)\), full torsion \(2\widetilde\Gamma^\rho{}_{[\mu\nu]}\), derivative index first, weighted brackets and curvature/Ricci contractions are stated in the PDF.
- BHL use different connection order and half-torsion. Their linear spin remains \(b_{\mu\nu}\); operational audit spin is \(s=-2b\). Only under the aligned ideal coupling is \(q=\kappa b^2=\kappa S_H^2/4\).
- The transformed factor \(g^{\alpha\beta}-u^\alpha u^\beta\) in the derivative source is not silently replaced by a spatial projector.
- Interface normal points outward, jumps are exterior minus interior, Gaussian normal distance has unit radial derivative, and the full-curvature condition is stronger than a metric no-thin-shell condition.
- No raw coefficient from modern kinetic spin hydrodynamics or EC–Dirac is substituted into the Weyssenhoff equations.
