# Unresolved assumptions, contradictions and implementation blocks

1. **Averaging is a material approximation.** A zero mean spin tensor is insufficient to discard velocity–spin, shear–spin, acceleration–spin, derivative or metric correlations. A full retained-correlation theory needs its moments and their evolution. No such closure has been invented here.
2. **Exact full symmetry differs from metric symmetry.** A nonzero coherent convective Frenkel spin tensor is excluded by strong full-field FLRW symmetry. A flat metric-only aligned-spin branch is verified, but its matter and torsion are not isotropic; it is not a counterexample to the strong-symmetry statement. A coherent spherical SO(3) radial axial spin field is allowed, but parity and center regularity restrict it. It is not a homogeneous unpolarized fermion gas.
3. **The generic spherical conserved-spin dust extension is not solver-ready.** For q=κS_H²/4 with q∝n² and ρ∝n^(1+w), c_eff²=[w(1+w)ρ−2q]/[(1+w)ρ−2q]. Low-spin ordinary dust has negative c_eff². The enthalpy-zero surface and high-spin superluminal effective branch need a different, independently justified physical treatment, not a numerical patch. This does not invalidate the symmetry-restricted FLRW ODE and is not a general theorem against EC bounces.
4. **The full microscopic Weyssenhoff matter action has not been independently reconstructed.** The audit selects the established Obukhov–Korotky/BHL field formulation. It verifies its signature translation and symmetry reduction. It does not substitute a made-up director action. Source variation normalization, connection-index ordering and spin coefficients must remain attached to each formulation.
5. **A finite star requires an exterior problem.** Effective metric Darmois conditions against vacuum require zero effective boundary pressure for a comoving no-flux interface. Homogeneous ordinary dust with nonzero spin at the edge fails that condition. A smooth spin-free collar is a conditional route, not a constructed solution.
6. **A fixed positive-w compact vacuum edge is itself nontrivial in GR.** The comoving Euler lapse can diverge logarithmically as ρ→0. No artificial atmosphere, pressure floor or free-boundary prescription is supplied.
7. **Full spinful junction conditions are not reproduced.** AKP's reference and publisher abstract were verified; its complete component-level interface formalism was not reconstructed from the accessible material. A surface-layer stress/spin action and distributional regularity choices are missing. This part must remain unimplemented.
8. **Vaidya requires radiation.** A changing interior mass is not enough to choose a null-radiation exterior. Interface flux, surface stresses and/or a noncomoving boundary need explicit equations.
9. **Effective dust is not ordinary dust.** Luz–Mena–Ziaie impose p_eff=0 by a pressure cancellation, unlike p=0 and P=-q. Their initial-data and nonsingularity statements cannot be transferred to our different EOS. Popławski's thermodynamic/particle-production assumptions also differ from the conserved-spin constant-w system.
10. **No strong hyperbolicity or global existence theorem is claimed for a future constrained scheme.** Acoustic reality is a necessary material check, not a complete proof of gauge/boundary well-posedness. Dust, shocks, caustics, chart necks and hyperbolicity-domain boundaries each need their own treatment.
11. **Interior diagnostics do not establish a global black-hole spacetime.** Event horizons, global regularity, eternal expansion, eternal oscillation and complete exterior matching cannot be inferred from finite sampled shell trajectories.
12. **No novelty claim.** Geometry and classical collapse identities are established. The audit's EOS derivative and symmetry-reduction calculations are conditional mathematical deductions, not a newly established physical prediction.

The supported next implementation target is a selected GR spherical initial-boundary benchmark. The generic Weyssenhoff PDE, spinful interface and additional transport models stay blocked. No existing FLRW equations or runtime files were changed.
