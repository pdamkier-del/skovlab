## Priorities that follow from the results

1. **Define the matter state before claiming a physical collapse prediction.** Decide the classical benchmark class or a renormalized quantum/kinetic approximation and establish its conserved currents, correlations, energy conditions and regime of validity. A local positive-frequency prescription in arbitrary dynamic geometry cannot be assumed without a state construction. The previous [matter review](../ec-matter-models/) remains relevant.
2. **Control numerical conservation and initial/boundary geometry.** The new tracker exposes reconstruction/continuity error instead of hiding it. Improve/retest that error, solve asymptotic compact-data constraints, establish outer treatment and perform both domain-size and resolution studies before interpreting a long contraction/expansion phase.
3. **Declare observables and end conditions in advance.** Choose current frame, material labels, reference surfaces and clocks. Record phase-resolved local/directional/domain e-folds, their errors, curvature/torsion invariants, null expansions, validity criteria and termination reasons. A finite constraint residual or one turning cell does not certify a global result.
4. **Compare actions consistently.** For a torsion attribution, reconstruct constrained data separately for ECD and Einstein–Dirac at matched physical free data. Track the same defined observables; do not assume that changing the contact leaves all source/geometric data fixed.
5. **State the possible outcomes honestly.** A theorem for a restricted class, a converged regular local turn, absence of a turn in a controlled interval, or an unresolved validity stop are all useful. No workflow should select initial conditions merely until a desired bounce or e-fold target appears.

This report expands the general analysis and supplies passive local diagnostics. It does not claim that an open research problem has been fully solved by a finite set of numerical examples.
