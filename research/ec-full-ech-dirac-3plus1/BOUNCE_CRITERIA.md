# What would count as a bounce?

For the normal congruence Theta_n=-K, a_i=D_i ln N, vorticity zero, the metric Raychaudhuri equation is

    n(Theta_n)=D²N/N-K_ij K^ij-(rho+S)/(2M²).

At K=0 positive derivative requires D²N/N>sigma²+(rho+S)/(2M²); the constraint also demands R3=2rho/M²+sigma². A lapse acceleration term is foliation dependent. It alone is not a material bounce. The canonical scalar contributes rho_phi+S_phi=2Pi²-2V: a potential can defocus despite its NEC. General inhomogeneous bounce criteria cannot be replaced by a flat-FLRW NEC slogan.

For a regular timelike material current u=j/sqrt(-j²), metric Raychaudhuri gives

    u(Theta_u)=-Theta_u²/3-sigma_u²+omega_u²
              -(rho_u+3P_u)/(2M²)+div a_u.

No torsion-autoparallel assumption is made. A candidate requires a proper-time contraction/expansion transition over an interval, finite metric and full connection curvature, regular current and tilt, converged constraints, controlled quantum sources and interaction attribution relative to separately constrained controls.
