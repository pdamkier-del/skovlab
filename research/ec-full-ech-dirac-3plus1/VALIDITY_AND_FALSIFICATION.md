# Physical validity and falsification

A. Exact classical variational results: action, 24-component elimination, full interaction stress, coupled equations, general ADM projections, shifted scalar momentum, normal/material bounce criteria. Independent local variation and geometric tests support these statements.

B. Actual numerical evidence: mixed inhomogeneous initial constraints, initial full-Cartan curvature, local coupled matter conservation. These do not constitute a spacetime bounce or a universal no-go theorem.

C. Conditional interpretation: beta=.25,c=10000,M=1,m=.1 define one classical model. Small classical fields and finite initial invariants do not by themselves establish quantum EFT control. Any physical interpretation needs a specified fermionic state, renormalized composite A² and stress, stress fluctuations, occupancy/Pauli bounds, all relevant physical scales relative to an independently justified cutoff, and omitted-operator/anomaly estimates. Products of classical mean currents cannot replace quantum four-point operators. The local chiral rotation is not an anomaly calculation.

D. Unknown: existence of a physically controlled torsion-attributable bounce in this model or broader quantum states. No such solution is demonstrated. Prior PR #5 excludes specific Gaussian/coherent constructions only; it supplies no theorem for all fermion states.

Falsification gates: reject a candidate if it fails original H=z or tetrad trace; loses constraints under spatial/time refinement; is a lapse/coordinate turn without material expansion; has singular current, tilt or Cartan invariants; depends on unphysical occupation or large uncontrolled fluctuations; or survives unchanged when the proposed driving interaction is removed in separately constrained controls. Potential/closed-curvature turns must be distinguished from torsion attribution. Do not start an expensive full 3D bounce search until a controlled source model and well-posed evolution reduction are supplied.
