# When could minimal torsion matter?

Use $\hbar=c=1$, reduced $\bar M_P=2.435\times10^{18}\,\mathrm{GeV}$, and

$$
G_A=\frac{3}{16\bar M_P^2}=3.16\times10^{-38}\ {\rm GeV}^{-2}.
$$

These are local microscopic momentum/density estimates, not a condition on a cloud's total mass. The input constants and arithmetic are reproduced in the validation record.

## Nuclear and interaction scales

At $n_0=0.16\,{\rm fm}^{-3}=1.229\times10^{-3}\,{\rm GeV}^3$, $m_n\simeq0.939\,{\rm GeV}$, an order-$n^2$ contact expectation gives

$$
\frac{|\Delta\epsilon|}{m_nn_0}
\sim \mathcal C\,\frac{G_A n_0}{m_n}
=4.14\times10^{-41}\mathcal C.
$$

Here $\mathcal C$ is a **state-dependent correlation factor**, not an adjustable bounce parameter. The illustrative nonrelativistic unpolarized free-sea calculation has $\mathcal C=3/2$, giving $6.21\times10^{-41}$; this is not a nuclear EOS calculation. Strong interactions and pairing change the correlator and ordinary energy. No controlled enhancement by forty orders of magnitude is established.

For perspective $G_F/G_A\simeq3.69\times10^{32}$, using $G_F=1.16638\times10^{-5}\,{\rm GeV}^{-2}$. This compares contact coefficients, not rates or equal physical effects: weak channels, phase space and equilibration times matter, and the Fermi approximation fails near the electroweak scale. Nuclear forces are not characterized by a universal weak-like constant.

## Why a sub-Planckian “rest-mass threshold” is misleading

Equating $\mathcal C G_A n^2$ to $mn$ while retaining a nonrelativistic gas gives

$$
n_{\rm NR}=\frac{m}{\mathcal C G_A}.
$$

For the preceding neutron-mass/free-sea illustration this is $1.98\times10^{37}\,{\rm GeV}^3$, with $p_F=(3\pi^2 n)^{1/3}\simeq8.37\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Although below the Planck momentum, it is about $8.9\times10^{12}$ times the assumed mass. The gas became relativistic vastly earlier; nucleon compositeness and the nuclear description also failed earlier. This extrapolation is **not** a justified torsion-dominance or bounce scale.

## Relativistic dimensional comparison

For a free ultrarelativistic reference with degeneracy $g$,

$$
n=\frac{g p_F^3}{6\pi^2},\qquad
\epsilon_0=\frac{g p_F^4}{8\pi^2}.
$$

If, conditionally, $|\langle J_5^2\rangle_{\rm ren}|=\mathcal C n^2$, then

$$
\frac{|\Delta\epsilon|}{\epsilon_0}
=\frac{g\mathcal C}{24\pi^2}\frac{p_F^2}{\bar M_P^2}.
$$

The coefficient $\mathcal C$ must be calculated for the relativistic state; the nonrelativistic value is **not** transferred here. With $g=2,\mathcal C=1$ only as a dimensional illustration, the ratio at $p_F=1$ GeV is $1.42\times10^{-39}$. Setting it to one gives $p_F\simeq10.9\bar M_P\simeq2.65\times10^{19}$ GeV. This is an extrapolation outside the controlled EFT, **not a predicted critical momentum**. Order-one factors change it, but the generic no-enhancement comparison is Planckian.

For independent unpolarized species, cross-current averages need not scale like the square of total number density. Do not infer an $N^2$ coherent enhancement merely by counting SM species. Coherence, connected correlations and any large-species cutoff analysis require separate calculations.

## Limits of the classical-spacetime claim

At relativistic momenta of order $\bar M_P$, the dimension-six expansion is no longer parametrically small. Gravitational loop effects, allowed higher-curvature/higher-dimension operators and the quantum state of geometry cannot generally be discarded. The curvature estimate $R\sim\epsilon/\bar M_P^2$ is also Planckian when $\epsilon\sim\bar M_P^4$. The cutoff can be lower in particular backgrounds or models; there is no theorem of classical EC validity up to an arbitrarily high density.

Large nonminimal couplings can lower a contact-interaction scale in other published theories, but introduce independent parameters and require their own cutoff/constraint analysis. They are not the fixed minimal theory investigated here.

**Reliable result:** ordinary dense-matter scales support a perturbative search for an extremely small torsion correction. They do not presently support a controlled prediction that minimal EC reverses collapse before quantum-gravity/EFT limitations become relevant.
