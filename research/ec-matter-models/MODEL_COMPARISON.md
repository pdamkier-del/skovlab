Framework numbers refer to the [literature review](LITERATURE_REVIEW.md). “Usable” distinguishes a field action, an equilibrium EOS and a closed dynamical initial-value theory.

The previous result

$$
c_{\rm eff}^2=
\frac{w(1+w)\rho-2q}{(1+w)\rho-2q}
$$

belongs to a particular EOS and conserved-spin fluid truncation. ECD does not impose that truncation. Conversely, a null microscopic Dirac front cone does not imply positive collective compressibility or stable hydrodynamic sound. The explicitly computed free-sea correction is attractive in its stated normal-state approximation; a more fundamental action is not an automatic stability cure.

## Ordinal research ranking

Lower numbers mean stronger fit for the indicated criterion; ties are allowed. These are scoped judgments, not numerical measures of correctness or proofs of well-posedness.

| 1. ECD / ECD–Maxwell | 4 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 |

| 3. Nuclear/QCD matter with EC matching | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| 4. Kinetic spin hydrodynamics | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 |

Framework 3's ordinary QCD/nuclear sector is established; its EC axial-current matching is an unresolved extension, not an equally established completed model. Framework 2 ranks highest in physical coverage but lowest in direct project feasibility. Framework 1 is easiest precisely because it omits much realistic dense-matter physics.

**Primary:** framework 2 as a minimal EC–SM effective theory, beginning with framework 3's controlled many-body matching subproblem. **Backup:** framework 1, a specified classical ECD–Maxwell benchmark (neutral ECD when charge is irrelevant).
