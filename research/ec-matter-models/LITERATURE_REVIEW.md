# Matter models for Einstein–Cartan collapse

Research date: 13 November 2026. This stage replaces the proposed full 3+1 investigation with a limited matter/action audit. No evolution, geometry derivation or simulator change is included. The [previous Weyssenhoff results](../ec-spherical/full-weyssenhoff/MATHEMATICAL_VIABILITY.md) remain unchanged.

**Finding:** the strongest physical foundation is **minimal EC coupled to Standard Model fermions, treated as a low-energy effective theory and evaluated in an interacting many-body state**. At nuclear density its material realization needs nuclear/QCD matching, not free classical spinors. There is no verified, ready-to-use dense-matter EC equation of state establishing a bounce in the sources inspected.

## Four established frameworks and the missing links

| Framework | Particles, interactions and local spin | Spatial variation and assumptions | Use for collapse |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Minimal EC–Dirac, optionally gauge-invariant Maxwell coupling** [1–3] | Specified Dirac species, masses, gravity; QED when included. The totally antisymmetric spin current is dual to the axial fermion current. Eliminating auxiliary torsion gives a fixed axial-current contact interaction. | Fields and spin bilinears can vary freely in space. Classical commuting spinors are a field idealization, not a filled Fermi sea. A quantum version requires a state and renormalized composite operators. | Best small mathematical benchmark. Dirac spin dynamics comes from its field equation. It omits strong interactions and bulk degeneracy statistics unless supplied through a quantum many-body treatment. |
| **2. EC with Standard Model matter** [2,3] | Quarks, leptons, gauge fields and Higgs/Yukawa interactions. The spin source is the **total** fermionic Lorentz current; SM neutrinos are chiral, not automatically massive Dirac fields. | Local quantum fields permit inhomogeneity. We select minimal torsion couplings and no Holst term. Published general EC–matter actions also allow nonminimal coefficients; those are different models. | Most complete starting action for ordinary matter. Finite-density QCD, reactions, transport and semiclassical stress must still be calculated; the action is not a closed stellar EOS. |
| **3. QCD-based dense nuclear/quark matter** [4–6] | Nuclear EFT describes nucleons and two-/three-nucleon interactions at low momenta; perturbative QCD describes quarks/gluons at sufficiently high chemical potential. Leptons, neutrality and weak equilibrium/transport are separate material ingredients. | Local thermodynamics can represent slowly varying matter within its validity range. A cold beta-equilibrated EOS is not a finite-temperature, neutrino-trapped collapse model. Intermediate-density phases remain uncertain. | Established realistic **matter sector**, not an already validated full EC collapse model. Adding the minimal EC contact operator requires matching the fundamental axial current, including multi-nucleon operators and its correlator. An ordinary EOS table alone does not provide this. |
| **4. Kinetic-theory spin hydrodynamics** [7,8] | Particle distributions, spin polarization/potential, collision/transport information and a chosen definition of spin current. | Derived near-equilibrium/gradient assumptions and microscopic matching matter. Spin and density can vary spatially. | Promising coarse graining: specific theories have causal, linearly stable equilibrium modes under coefficient conditions. They are not a theorem about arbitrary dense EC collapse; torsion coupling, dense-matter coefficients and validity must be established. |

Framework 3's QCD calculations and framework 4's kinetic equations are established. Their proposed coupling/matching to our EC collapse problem is **additional work**, not an existing completed model inferred from those papers.

“Minimal” specifies our tree-level benchmark at a matching scale. It is not a claim that symmetry forbids all other EC couplings or that quantum corrections need no counterterms. Those distinctions become essential before a high-energy extrapolation.

## Which interactions belong where?

| Ingredient | Fundamental action | Dense-matter approximation / importance |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | QCD quark–gluon coupling | Nuclear forces, three-body forces, pairing and nuclear/quark phases dominate much neutron-star microphysics. Nucleon models are matched approximations to QCD, not pointlike fundamental neutron Dirac fields at arbitrary momentum. |
| Electromagnetic | QED / SM gauge covariant derivative and gauge kinetic term | Charge neutrality, electrons/muons, screening and magnetic polarization. Bulk neutrality does not remove electromagnetic physics. |
| Weak | Electroweak gauge and Yukawa interactions | Sets composition, beta conversion and neutrino transport on density-, temperature- and timescale-dependent regimes. A beta-equilibrium EOS must be justified relative to collapse time. |
| Quantum statistics | Fermionic quantization and antisymmetry | Degeneracy pressure and exchange are state effects; they are not an extra classical force or supplied by one commuting spinor. |
| Torsion | Minimal EC connection plus fermion spin coupling | A universal dimension-six total-axial-current operator after eliminating torsion. Its dense-matter energy/pressure require quantum correlations; it is exceptionally weak at known nuclear scales. |

Gauge strengths use exterior derivatives, $F=dA$ and $\mathcal F=d\mathcal A+\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal A$. Replacing them by antisymmetrized torsionful spacetime derivatives would change/break the usual gauge theory [2]. We do not use the U(1)-breaking EC variants as standard electromagnetism.

## Primary sources and access limits

1. **Hehl–Datta (1971)**, *Nonlinear spinor equation and asymmetric connection in general relativity*, [doi:10.1063/1.1665738](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1665738). Original identification verified bibliographically; the original full text was not independently reproduced. The coefficient below is verified directly and against [2,3].
2. **Karananas–Shaposhnikov–Shkerin–Zell (2021)**, *Matter matters in Einstein–Cartan gravity*, [2106.13811v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.13811v2), Phys. Rev. D 104, 064036. Primary conventions, torsion elimination and gauge-field sections inspected. Their broad action permits extra couplings; our minimal specialization does not import them.
3. **Shaposhnikov–Shkerin–Timiryasov–Zell (2021)**, *Einstein–Cartan Portal to Dark Matter*, [2008.11686v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.11686v2), Phys. Rev. Lett. 126, 161301. Eqs. (1–2): minimal $\alpha=\beta=0$ gives $-3J_5^2/(16\bar M_P^2)$ in their mostly-plus conventions. Their dark-matter production scenario is not used as collapse evidence.
4. **Hebeler–Lattimer–Pethick–Schwenk (2013)**, *Equation of state and neutron star properties constrained by nuclear physics and observation*, [1303.4662](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.4662), Astrophys. J. 773, 11. Primary nuclear-EFT/EOS methodology inspected. Its high-density extensions are not uniformly ab-initio nuclear calculations or torsion correlators.
5. **Kurkela–Romatschke–Vuorinen (2010)**, *Cold Quark Matter*, [0912.1856v2](https://arxiv.org/html/0912.1856v2), Phys. Rev. D 81, 105021. Cold dense QCD through order $\alpha_s^2$, with a finite strange mass; not a controlled EOS at every neutron-star density.
6. **Annala et al. (2020)**, *Evidence for quark-matter cores in massive neutron stars*, [1903.09121v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.09121v2), [doi:10.1038/s41567-020-0914-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-020-0914-9). Primary abstract/methodological scope checked: nuclear/QCD/observation constraints do not remove interpolation or phase uncertainty.
7. **Weickgenannt (2023)**, *Linearly stable and causal relativistic first-order spin hydrodynamics*, [2307.13561v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.13561v2), Phys. Rev. D 108, 076011. Kinetic derivation and conditional linear stability/causality scope inspected.
8. **Xie et al. (2023)**, *Causality and stability analysis for the minimal causal spin hydrodynamics*, [2306.13880v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.13880v3), Phys. Rev. D 108, 094031. Earlier primary audit retained: finite-wave-number instabilities can survive endpoint checks in a different dissipative theory.
9. **Müller–Nowaczyk (2017)**, *A Universal Spinor Bundle and the Einstein–Dirac–Maxwell Equation as a Variational Theory*, [1504.01034v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.01034v3), Lett. Math. Phys. 107, 933–961. Classical torsion-free Cauchy-development framework; not a dense quantum EC stability theorem.
10. **Dolan (2010)**, *Chiral fermions and torsion in the early Universe*, [0911.1636v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0911.1636v3). Primary discussion of quantum/thermal averaging ambiguity inspected. Its homogeneous state assumptions are not imported as a universal EOS.

The action/coefficient are literature-established and independently checked. The free-gas exchange calculation in [ECD_DERIVATION.md](ECD_DERIVATION.md) is a conditional audit calculation for a precisely specified state. No source inspected establishes the desired realistic, causal, nonsingular collapse solution.
