| Proposed failure/claim | Test/result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong torsion sign or coefficient | Direct affine contraction and spin matrices; q=3κA | No material benchmark error found in corrected convention |
| Each chiral field is null, so interaction must vanish | Total square retains cross terms and is −n² | That objection fails for distinct fields |
| Stress was obtained by invalid on-shell variation | Independent off-shell lapse/axis variation, then Dirac substitution | Classical source passes |

| Any amplitude is a physical six-mode fermion state | Occupation≤1 fixes n0≤6/L³ | Arbitrarily large occupation interpretation rejected |

| Six modes and six species are equivalent | Same species gives −72, distinct gives −36 in V² units | Equivalence contradicted |

| Removing torsion with same H is a valid control | Separate action has different Hamiltonian constraint | Invalid comparison rejected; separately solved radiation control crunches |
| Homogeneous volume oscillations follow | v̈>0 in paired massless family | Repeated regular volume oscillations excluded in this family |
| Tiny mass test proves realistic massive matter | Initial field is not a prepared massive positive-energy state | Claim rejected |
| Bounce demonstrates inflation/baby universe | Exact accelerating phase only 0.347 e-fold; no cloud/exterior | Claim unsupported |

No numerical failure was hidden as a physical singularity. No change to the minimal action, adjustable torsion sign, new matter component or fluid closure was used to obtain a favorable outcome. The strongest present negative finding is physical scale/control, not destruction of the classical exact solution.
