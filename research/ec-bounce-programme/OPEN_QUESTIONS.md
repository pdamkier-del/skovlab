# Prioritized scientific questions after P07

1. What connected four-point contribution, beyond local Gaussian Hartree–Fock, is generated by a concrete admissible state, and can its error relative to the retained source be controlled? A merely self-consistent two-point truncation does not answer this. P07 covers arbitrary coherent amplitudes in the specified parity-even Gaussian sector.
2. What is the exact full-covariance ↔ regular-current subtraction map in the proposed cosmological 2PI approximation? Recover a Hermitian full generator and a conserved metric-varied stress, including reference-projector derivatives and matched counterterms. The printed component defect cannot be interpreted as actual Pauli violation without that map.
3. Can the full renormalized stress and spacetime-smeared noise remain controlled at a candidate turn? The new fixed-cutoff mode data are not a continuum curved-space prediction.
4. Does a genuinely distinct polarized/parity-breaking or multispecies correlated state admit controlled enhancement? Do not silently extrapolate the parity-even single-species bound to it, or alter couplings merely until a bounce appears.
5. If a physical source passes these gates, does one constrained inhomogeneous perturbation survive with spatial/time convergence and a separately constrained torsion-free control? B6 remains unmet.
6. Can microscopic spin hydrodynamics be matched to this same renormalized state and correlator hierarchy rather than an independently assigned variance?
7. Only after specific earlier obstructions justify progression: identify a definite Holst/pseudoscalaron or extended action and audit its modes, signs and constraints. Those model families remain uninvestigated, not rejected.

No necessity of quantum gravity, universal EC no-go, finite-cloud bounce or baby universe is established.
