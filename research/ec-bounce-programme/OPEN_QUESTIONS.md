# Prioritized scientific questions

1. Can a specified minimal-coupling nonequilibrium fermion covariance and connected four-point function produce an order-one negative source under a demonstrably controlled renormalized expansion, with bounded stress noise? The present Gaussian bound does not cover that state class.
2. Does the cold/out-of-equilibrium route in the inspected nonminimal 2PI theory preserve Pauli occupation, its full positive kinetic/rest stress and a valid cutoff when equations and initial conditions are translated consistently? An apparent radiation-only cancellation is inadequate.
3. Can a hierarchy or resummation (including any proposed large-N limit) bound omitted terms and fluctuations at the actual turn? Conventional species-loop power counting alone is not a universal theorem.
4. What finite matching coefficients and spacetime-smeared stress-noise kernel are required for the same action/state in a weak-curvature regime? Flat vacuum Wick ordering is not a full curved-space solution.
5. If a source passes those gates, does one constraint-consistent inhomogeneous perturbation survive with independently checked spatial/time convergence and a separately constrained torsion-free control? This is mandatory B6 evidence.
6. Is there a microscopically matched spinfluid with additional correlations/transport that avoids the demonstrated same-state closure mismatch? An ideal spin-squared dilution law alone is insufficient.
7. Only after the above obstructions are clarified: which exact Holst/pseudoscalaron action from the broader project is intended, and what are its degrees of freedom/signs? No unique action was recovered in the inspected priority research records.

Not currently established: need for quantum gravity, impossibility of all EC theories, a realistic finite-cloud bounce, a baby universe, or post-bounce inflation.
