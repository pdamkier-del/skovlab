# States, subtraction and physical scope

## Massless class

Use g independent massless Weyl channels. One channel comprises a particle and its antiparticle, not a Dirac species: a Dirac field contains two opposite-chirality channels. States are homogeneous, isotropic, gauge-invariant quasifree (Gaussian), with no anomalous pairing, interchannel coherence or non-Gaussian four-point cumulant. Occupations 0≤f_i±(p)≤1. A chirality sign s_i=±1 fixes its axial charge convention. The continuum thermodynamic limit is used; a finite torus with a few zero modes is not covered by a continuum density bound.

Thermal examples take f±=[exp((p∓μ)/T)+1]⁻¹. Charge asymmetry and total particle-plus-antiparticle density differ:

d_i=∫p²dp(f_i+−f_i−)/(2π²),
n_i,total=∫p²dp(f_i++f_i−)/(2π²),
ρ0=Σ_i∫p³dp(f_i++f_i−)/(2π²).

For thermal Weyl channels, d=μT²/6+μ³/(6π²), P0=7π²T⁴/360+μ²T²/12+μ⁴/(24π²), ρ0=3P0. μ may vanish or have either sign. μ=0 does not mean an empty gas.

The sharp cutoff theorem separately assumes compact support p≤Λ in every occupied channel. Untruncated thermal tails have no compact support; numerical thermal integrals run to infinity and are not silently put under that theorem. A physical EFT treatment of their tails would need a stated tolerance and matching above its validity scale.

## Massive class

A distinct test uses unpolarized free Dirac species with mass m, ordinary vector chemical potential μ and f±=[exp((√(p²+m²)∓μ)/T)+1]⁻¹. It includes both antiparticles and the positive fermion rest energy. Its medium scalar condensate is S=2∫d³p/(2π)³(m/E)(f++f−), net charge D=2∫d³p/(2π)³(f+−f−). It is not a coherently paired or interacting many-body state.

## Renormalization and noise

Fix the flat-space Minkowski vacuum Wick polynomial at this order, with vacuum energy subtracted and the minimal contact coefficient matched at the declared reference scale. This is a precise reference prescription for the calculations, not a generally covariant renormalization of the complete interacting stress in a dynamical geometry. Massless conformal FLRW reference modes can be transported freely; anomalies, interaction-induced state changes and curved-space counterterms are not eliminated by that observation.

Renormalization of the interacting EFT permits operator mixing and local gravitational/matter counterterms. Their finite matching data, higher-dimensional operators and errors must be specified before a physical bounce claim. We have not chosen finite counterterms to engineer a turn. An arbitrary scheme-dependent contact expectation alone is not a physical prediction without consistently matched stress and couplings.

The finite Fock test computes ⟨Q⟩ and Var(Q) exactly in its regulated mode space. These variances include vacuum fluctuations and are not renormalized spacetime-smeared stress noise. The continuum free total-energy variance per volume is also recorded from ∫p⁴ f(1−f)dp/(2π²). Neither is a demonstration of small metric/stress fluctuations in a bouncing spacetime. Required smearing window: microscopic correlation length ≪ smearing scale ≪ background curvature/evolution scale, with all momenta below the justified EFT range. No such window at a candidate bounce has been established here.

Free conformal thermal evolution and equilibrium thermodynamics are different approximations. A conserved formal EOS does not prove rapid equilibration or solve the Schwinger–Keldysh hierarchy. No interacting dynamical evolution is claimed.
