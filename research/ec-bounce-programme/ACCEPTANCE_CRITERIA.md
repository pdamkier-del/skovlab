A PASS on reference-state B2 does not transfer to a conjectured strong quantum state. Algebraic consistency or fourth-order classical convergence cannot replace B3/B6. A Hubble zero or zero full Ricci scalar alone cannot certify regularity. Never count a solver failure as singularity evidence.

Homogeneous flat candidate constraints would be H²=κρ/3; any free/torsion-free control must solve this with its own ρ. New numerical work here consists of operator matrices, quadratures and symbolic identities; spatial and temporal evolution refinements are not applicable. No claim of a genuinely inhomogeneous assessment is made.

Stopping classification: **STOP C — bounded execution handoff**, not STOP A (no Level B) or STOP B (all justified EC models are not exhausted). Further calculations require a specified dynamical quantum approximation and control data, not an expensive solver built around an assumed source.
