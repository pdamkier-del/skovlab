Prior results preserved: the six-classical-field exact bounce is CONDITIONAL as a physical candidate, with verified mathematical regularity/completeness and limited homogeneous robustness. It fails to supply controlled quantized matter/B3 and does not satisfy B6. The local analytical single-field turn also exists in its torsion-free control, so is not a demonstrated torsion-driven mechanism. The one-field 3D contraction studies are finite tested trajectories, not a quantum no-go theorem.

Progression rule: weak Gaussian failure justifies checking its omitted correlations/closure and a published explicit action. It does not justify skipping to arbitrary higher-curvature models. Broad Model 1 is not marked exhausted merely because its tractable branch is restricted.
