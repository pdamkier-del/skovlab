THEORY & VALIDITY

A specified effective model, with visible limits.

Equations retained

FLRW: H² = A a⁻³⁽¹⁺ʷ⁾ − B a⁻⁶; ä/a = −½(1+3w)A a⁻³⁽¹⁺ʷ⁾ + 2B a⁻⁶. In the bounce-centred benchmark A is fixed by H(0)=0. A bounce is therefore an initial condition, not an independent prediction.

KS: HΩ² + 1/Y² + 2HᵣHΩ = ρ − S. 2Ÿ/Y + HΩ² + 1/Y² = −pₑff; Ẍ/X + Ÿ/Y + HᵣHΩ = −pₑff. Here ρ ∝ (XY²)⁻⁽¹⁺ʷ⁾, S ∝ (XY²)⁻² and pₑff = wρ − S.

Time, units & interpretation

All curves use τ = t/t*, with c = 1 and L* = t*. Rates are shown in t*⁻¹, derivatives in t*⁻², and curvature in t*⁻⁴. There is no fixed conversion to seconds without specifying a physical density scale. KS scales X, Y and aᵥ are plotted relative to L*. KS uses 8πGρt*²; FLRW density coefficients absorb 8πG/3.

FLRW benchmark spans [−T,T]; other starts and KS span [0,T]. E-folds count from the first sample; accelerated e-folds accumulate only when H > 0 and ä/a > 0. For KS every mean quantity uses aᵥ = (XY²)⅓.

Numerics & assumptions

Adaptive RK4 with step doubling, explicit constraints, geometric curvature invariants and tolerance comparison. Hover values are numerical samples; event times use refined zero crossings. No stopping threshold proves a singularity.

Einstein–Cartan torsion and effective spin repulsion motivate the model. Isotropic spin averaging, the barotropic closure and conserved particle number are modelling assumptions. A computed turning point is a numerical result. A baby universe or a resolved realistic black-hole interior would be a speculative interpretation.
